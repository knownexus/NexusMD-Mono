Dashboard — what looks unimplemented or restrictive

Select the issues that bother you most (pick as many as you like)

A

Search bar does nothing

The ⌘K / search input in the topbar is a visual placeholder. Typing anything has no effect.

B

Sparklines & trend badges are fake

The mini charts and "on track / +2" trend labels on all four stat cards are hardcoded arrays, not real history.

C

Widget layout is rigid

Wide widgets (Sprint, Team) are permanently pinned to the left column; narrow widgets (Review, Blockers, etc.) are permanently pinned to the right. Drag-to-reorder only shuffles within each column — you can't mix them.

D

Quick links are hardcoded

The UAT / Dev / Cirrus HR links are baked into the code. There's no way to add, remove, or rename them from the UI.

E

"On track" is always on track

The green "On track" pill in the Sprint widget is hardcoded — it never shows "At risk" or "Behind" regardless of burndown data.

F

Greeting is time-unaware

"Good morning, Phillip" — always says morning, never afternoon or evening.

G

Recent Activity widget is passive

Shows a feed but items are not clickable — you can't navigate to the PR/work item from there.
